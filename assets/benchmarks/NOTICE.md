## Original UI fixtures

The 48 `ui-*.png` mock screenshots were created for this project by
`tools/generate_pca_ui.ps1` and are licensed under GPL-3.0-only, like the
application. `ui-manifest.json` records their hashes and provenance. They contain
original generic layouts and synthetic placeholder content, with no screen
capture, user files, personal details, product logos, or copied application art.
They are used only for unlabeled PCA basis learning.
